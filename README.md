### 👨‍💻 Professional Statement

Principal Consultant &amp; SAP BTP Architect with 11+ years shipping cloud-native enterprise software at scale across Wipro, Capgemini, IBM, and TCS. Led the zero-to-production delivery of **STO360** — an SAP-certified Oil & Gas product on SAP BTP — and won 1st Place Americas in SAP Hack2Build with a RAP-based S/4HANA clean-core extension.

Deep expertise spans **SAP CAP (Node.js)**, **Python microservices**, **ABAP/RAP**, **SAP HANA Cloud**, **Event Mesh**, and **XSUAA security** — now extended with generative AI: LLMs, Gemma models, RAG, and HANA Vector Engine for document-heavy workflows.

> **At the intersection of SAP + AI**, I design cloud-native enterprise systems and build open-source tools for the AI era — Chhanda (local-first LLM gateway), lgit (semantic version control), and an on-device AI collab ecosystem.
